# Almost Fall — Golden Creative Benchmark  ·  STATUS: CLOSED (owner-approved calibration)

*Frozen 2026-08-12 by owner direction. This is a **calibration reference**, NOT top-level canon and
NOT exact expected output. It is a `reference` artifact under `docs/` (like CATEGORY_TAXONOMY.md /
NAMING_CONVENTIONS.md), not an eighth locked-set document.*

## What this is — and is not

- **IS:** the owner-approved reference for the *quality and structural judgment* a fresh
  `/new-campaign` run should independently reach — a calibration bar.
- **IS NOT:** an exact template to reproduce. Individual collection choices, rail titles, content
  headlines and the Default order below are **representative examples**, not immutable strings. A
  fresh run that produces *different but comparable-or-better* judgment through the proper owner
  checkpoints is a success; one that merely re-emits these exact words is not the test.
- **Provenance:** distilled from the historical Almost Fall run (`cmp_01KZCD765E6NNW2TZR1AFHQMWG`)
  as *candidate source material*. The historical run's own state, approvals, and review books are
  **untouched and are not authority**; this benchmark neither modifies nor resumes them.

## Sole future use

Evaluate whether a fresh `/new-campaign` run independently produces campaign judgment of
**comparable or better** quality via the proper owner checkpoints (Architecture Review → Final
Campaign Spec approval). The acceptance question is *not* "did it recreate Almost Fall?" but "did it
independently earn campaign judgment at or above this bar, through the guided workflow?"

No further Almost Fall creative review until that fresh E2E run. This benchmark is **CLOSED**.

## Resolved structural principles (the load-bearing part)

These are owner-resolved and govern how a fresh run is judged. The detailed selection rule is the
**Content Charter `merchandising.collection_breadth` (CB-1..CB-5)**; the numeric launch floor is the
**Campaign Charter `render_003`**. This benchmark does not restate or compete with either.

1. **Strong campaign frame first.** A real tension + a disagreeable point of view + one primary
   behavior, clearing the anti-slop rules (no "the turn", no pumpkin/cosy, no discount framing, no
   `The ___` template ≥40%). Almost Fall's frame — *"Fall's in every window, not yet in the
   forecast. Save what you want for cooler days."* — is the calibration example.
2. **Role-earned breadth, not a count (CB-1..CB-4).** Each durable collection passes the three CB-1
   gates (canonical category · genuine campaign/vertical fit · distinct shopper/product job).
   Durable reusable catalog value strengthens the case (CB-3) but is not a fourth gate and never
   rescues an off-premise/redundant/quota collection. **No target/max/per-vertical count, no vertical
   symmetry.** The count below (22) is an *outcome*, never a goal.
3. **Unequal vertical conviction is the design.** Fashion = spine · Home + Beauty = strong secondary
   · Travel = supporting adjacent · Outdoors = light counterpoint · **Tech = content/service only
   (zero durable collections)**. A vertical legitimately contributing zero collections is a correct
   outcome, not a gap.
4. **Selection ≠ fulfillment (CB-5).** The Wizard (with owner approval) decides which canonical
   collections belong; CollectionCuration decides whether enough valid product truth exists to
   launch them under `render_003`. Current low depth / sourcing difficulty is **fulfillment risk**,
   not a creative reason to drop a role-earned selection; passing `render_003` does not prove
   belonging.
5. **Category-shaped collection names + premise-native rails.** Collections are durable, legible
   taxonomy subjects; the campaign personality lives in the rails and content, at the voice bar.
6. **Cross-collection story rails are the intended creative.** Where the current backend cannot
   render XC, *generating the XC idea is a creative success*; the render gap is a tracked
   **product-capability** failure, not a creative one; the 1C fallback is acceptable degradation
   that never redefines the intended design.
7. **A composed Default that argues.** Spine → proof → selective supporting beats — not a
   representational rotation through every vertical.
8. **Taxonomy discipline.** Every collection/rail source resolves to a real canonical node; no
   invented merges; no "drop→rail" leaving a rail without a selected product source.

## Creative quality rubric — what a fresh `/new-campaign` must demonstrate

Judged on independent quality via owner checkpoints, **not** on reproducing these words:

1. A premise with real tension + disagreeable POV + one primary behavior; passes anti-slop.
2. Role-earned collections under unequal conviction (CB-1..CB-4); a zero-collection vertical is a
   valid outcome; product abundance alone never qualifies; count is emergent.
3. Category-shaped collection names + premise-native rails/content at the voice bar.
4. Cross-collection story rails expressed as the intended creative (render gap tracked separately).
5. Content that earns its place — situational, ownable, real query intent; SEO title and card voice
   may differ; no "Why Everything Is…", "X Is the New Y", "Decoded", "Best X 2026" unless earned.
6. A composed Default that argues the conviction hierarchy, not a six-vertical rotation.
7. Taxonomy discipline; selection ≠ fulfillment kept distinct (`render_003` is a separate feasibility
   check, not a creative pass/fail).
