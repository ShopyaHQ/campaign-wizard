# PHASE 1 CAMPAIGN OUTPUT REQUIREMENT — owner-locked 2026-08-07
Product-scope ruling + permanent process model. This is part of what a COMPLETED CAMPAIGN
MEANS — current process SSOT, not next-pass material.

## The four parallel outputs (Default + six verticals)
Verticals: Fashion · Home & Interior · Tech & Electronics · Beauty & Grooming ·
Travel & Luggage · Outdoors & Sports.
1. STRATEGY — central premise + per-vertical evidence-backed interpretations (shared premise;
   mechanism may differ per vertical; never force one vertical's reasoning onto another).
2. MERCHANDISING — per vertical >=3 rail/collection pairs, each with: thesis, role, inclusion/
   exclusion logic, >=24 dependable live members (>50 preferred where reality supports it,
   never padded), 12 pins, ordering, names/descriptions/copy, annotations, availability +
   execution readiness. Scale reporting distinguishes rail pins vs collection memberships vs
   UNIQUE products; cross-collection reuse is legitimate only when the product independently
   satisfies both theses.
3. EDITORIAL/CONTENT — per vertical >=3 distinct content concepts (ideation floor) + campaign-
   wide/default concepts; no generic SEO filler. Per approved concept: working title, thesis,
   why-now, signals, target surface, reader question/tension, takeaway, related collections/
   rails/example products, justified format, length/depth, research needs, production-ready
   generation prompt, 2-3 headlines, dek direction, cover brief, placement, execution status
   (idea|approved|prompt-ready|authored|published). The wizard may stop at prompt-ready and
   NEVER claims handoff content is authored/published. Content rails are distinct objects from
   product rails (worth opening vs worth browsing/saving); content may be campaign-wide,
   vertical-specific, or reusable; no repetition across all six filters.
4. EXECUTION — one master product/collection/rail CSV (campaign, target surface/filter,
   collection, product, live membership, is_rail_item, rail name/position, execution facts);
   content handoff package SEPARATE from the product CSV unless implementation evidence shows
   a shared contract; other seam handoffs; execution manifest.

## Permanent Phase 1 process model (from kickoff — NOT an optional post-campaign expansion)
premise -> vertical interpretations -> product AND editorial opportunity development IN
PARALLEL -> collection/rail architecture + content program -> owner architecture/content
review -> inventory reality tests + content feasibility -> targeted sourcing where necessary
-> naming/copy + approved article briefs/prompts -> product assembly + content selections ->
Default/All composition -> master execution package + content handoffs -> final owner
acceptance.

CRITICAL PRINCIPLE: editorial/content is NOT added after merchandising. Within each vertical
the same premise, evidence, shopper tension and category interpretation generate BOTH product
opportunities (theses, rails, merchandising logic) AND editorial opportunities (articles,
guides, content rails, questions worth answering) — each informs the other during
conceptualization. A campaign reaches the owner architecture checkpoint with BOTH the proposed
rail/collection architecture AND the proposed editorial/content program.

## Required per-vertical output
Evidence-backed interpretation · >=3 product rail/collection concepts · >=3 distinct content
concepts · rationale linking both to the same interpretation · inventory feasibility ·
content feasibility · owner-approved product architecture · owner-selected content program ·
naming and copy · production-ready prompts for approved content · final product assembly.

## Default / All composition (formal E2E step, not technical aggregation)
Default/All is NOT a seventh vertical — no vertical interpretation applies. It is a separate
required deliverable, decided LATE (after the six vertical programs exist): selected/prominent
rails; global ordering; category representation; campaign-wide/default content; vertical-
specific content excluded from Default; overall editorial balance.
After vertical programs are developed, deliberately decide: which rails deserve prominence on
All; global priority/order; which verticals are represented; which campaign-wide/default
editorial appears; what stays vertical-specific; what the combined Default experience
communicates.

## Owner rulings 2026-08-07 (R1-R5)
R1 feed capacity: the campaign-global explore_feeds<=4 model is INVALID for Phase 1. Minimum
   3 product rails per required vertical; no replacement arbitrary global number; the frontend
   audit establishes whether a practical per-surface maximum is needed.
R2 category vocabulary: outdoors_sports is a distinct REQUIRED vertical. Never mapped to
   wellness_health (which may remain as its own category).
R3 collection reuse (desired product rule, NOT current backend permission): a durable
   collection may support multiple rail placements/surfaces when the same thesis genuinely
   applies; collections are not duplicated merely for a different surface/hook; each placement
   may carry its own surface, rail title, copy, ordering. Audit identifies the implementation
   change before anything depends on it.
R4 editorial capacity: S5 cap 2 is incompatible with Phase 1 and is NOT the future limit; the
   Sanity/frontend audit determines the smallest content implementation model. Wizard
   ideation/prompt output is never capped by current rendering capacity.
R5 vertical interpretation: one central premise + six evidence-backed vertical
   interpretations; architecture and content derive from the relevant interpretation; carried
   in the existing brief/process, no new workflow state.
Renderability rule: placements the frontend cannot yet render are marked
'required — technically blocked' — the requirement is never weakened to match implementation.

## Content placement binding (owner spec clarification, 2026-08-07)
The final campaign seams are: pinned rail items, collections, AND content. Every content piece
carries seam-level bindings as a first-class deliverable: (a) content-rail placement and
position per surface; (b) the planned collection(s) it belongs to/spotlights; (c) the CONCRETE
verified products it incorporates (named, priced, from live membership; pending bindings follow
their collection's sourcing). Formats include editorials, explainers, guides, reviews and
single-product SPOTLIGHTS. A prompt is not complete until bindings exist; the placement map is
part of the content handoff package. Retroactive for Almost Fall; standard from kickoff.

## Collections depth rule (owner, 2026-08-07)
Every vertical generates AT LEAST 5 collections (>=3 railed product rails stay the rail
minimum; the remainder may be collection-only — a collection needs no rail). Rationale: a
related-collections rail on a filtered view must have enough collection cards to fill it.
Collections-entity rails are the mechanism (frontend-supported feed entity). TENSION for
charter ruling: render_002 sets collections-feed first-paint quality minimum at 10; owner
floor is 5 — quality_exception or 5-floor/10-target ruling needed at next charter revision.
CONTENT SCALING: the ideation floor scales with collections — minimum one PAIRED content
concept per launched collection (generated in the same pass from the same interpretation),
replacing the flat 3-per-vertical floor.
