## The moment

Tomorrow is Risshū — the old calendar's first day of autumn — and it is still summer-hot
`SIG-014`. Retail and editorial have fully turned: current pieces frame the moment as "summer
swiftly winding down" and report early fall outerwear already selling out `SIG-006`. The
shopper is inside the gap at its widest point: everything being sold reads fall, nothing fall
is wearable, and the wardrobe she has is three months old.

## The crossing

The trade's own urgency ("decide on fall now, it's selling out") collides with the weather's
refusal to cooperate. For a shopper, the honest verb for a fall piece in August heat is not
*wear* and — given the mood against accumulation `SIG-011` `SIG-012` — often not *buy* either.
It is *save*. The platform's core gesture is the correct response to the moment's central
frustration. That is the structural finding of this scan.
