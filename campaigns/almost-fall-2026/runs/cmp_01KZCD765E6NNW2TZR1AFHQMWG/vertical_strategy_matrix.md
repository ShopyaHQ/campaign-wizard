## 2 · HOME & INTERIOR
Why it matters: the home turns to fall before the weather too — textiles, palettes and candles
ship in August; nesting desire precedes any cold night to justify it.
Evidence: FW26 home forecasts — chocolate brown/terracotta/moss called the "new core
neutrals," burgundy/plum accents; merino wool blankets +205% YoY search; Neo Deco +210%;
textural contrast (linen + wool + rattan + dark wood + brass); broad shift from stark
minimalism to "warm, collected" rooms. (Trendalytics FW26 home report.)
Tension: it's 85F — you can't light the fire, but you want the room to feel like October.
An August wool blanket is aspiration, not utility. That IS the save.
Interpretation: in home the gap is SENSORY — fall arrives as texture and colour before
temperature. Save the heavy layer for the first cold night; adopt the palette now through
objects that don't need cold to make sense.
Merchandising territories: (a) the first blanket — wool/cashmere layers [wait]; (b) fall's
palette in usable objects — chocolate/terracotta/moss ceramics, glass, cushions, brass
[bridge]; (c) one-object shifts — a candle, a lamp, a bowl [transform].
Editorial territories: why every room is turning brown (colour story); the case for buying
blankets in August; the unlit candle — wanting fall as scent.
Exclusions/weak: pumpkin/harvest cliche decor excluded; RL-Christmas/holiday signals (+5,900%)
are real but OUT OF PREMISE (fall, not holiday); whimsical-decor direction off-tone; big
furniture is a maybe (classic save behavior but heavy for rails).

## 5 · TRAVEL & LUGGAGE
Why: fall trips are booked in August — shoulder season is the premise inverted into time; the
trip you want belongs to a later month and saving/planning IS the behavior.
Evidence: Sept-Oct fares reported at seasonal price floors; industry coverage of shoulder
season "becoming the primary season"; overtourism steering travellers to fall; premium demand
shifts when school starts. (ShopBack fall-2026 fare analysis; TRO; Afar.)
Tension (owner-corrected): the trip being planned now often belongs to a cooler, later
season. Unlike fashion's open-ended wait, this save often has a booking-window deadline.
Interpretation: in travel the gap is TEMPORAL BY DEFINITION — wanted and planned in August,
taken in September/October. Save the trip-ready kit for a trip that has a date.
Merchandising territories: (a) the shoulder-season carry-on kit — luggage, organizers
[wait-with-date]; (b) pack-now pieces that work in both climates [bridge — legitimate R3
reuse candidate with Fashion B]; (c) the one-upgrade travel accent — the good passport
holder, the charger kit [transform — overlaps Tech legitimately].
Editorial territories: why September is the new July; the 10-day fall trip packing list;
where fall arrives first.
Exclusions/weak: the category's loudest signal is CHEAPNESS and discount language is BANNED —
frame as the smarter season, never the cheaper one; destination content must stay
product-adjacent (Shopya sells products, not trips).

## 6 · OUTDOORS & SPORTS  (distinct vertical per R2 — never wellness_health)

summer — the good months (Sept-Nov) are researched and saved for in August.

Tension (owner-corrected, geography-safe): late summer is when many shoppers begin preparing

first-cold-morning readiness is the goal.
Interpretation: in outdoors the gap is PREPARATION — August is the research-and-save window
for the season the category actually lives for. Save the system; wear the shoulder pieces now;
one piece converts a summer kit.
Merchandising territories: (a) the layering system — base/mid/puffy [wait/save-the-system];
(b) trail-to-street shoulder pieces usable now — trail runners, light shells [bridge];
(c) one-piece conversions — the merino tee, the shell refresh [transform].
Editorial territories: the layering system, explained (genuinely useful classic); repair &
reproof before fall (maintenance culture — trust-building, anti-filler); trail runners vs
boots for fall.
Exclusions/weak: fall team sports = school-adjacent = b2s risk, EXCLUDED; no supplements/
recovery content (that's wellness_health); "new gear season" hype tempered by the visible
repair culture — respect it.
